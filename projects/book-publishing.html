02 / The process

How I designed and made it

Planning & design

The planning and design aspect of this project is not a very complicated.

At the time we were creating this Donald Trump had just come into power as the US president so we decided to make a mash up of Hercules the Greek demigod and trump so we created a ladybird style book on the Hercules and the nemean lion story but having the main character not as Hercules but as trump so we called the book Trumpales. I am still not sure where the idea came for this project came from except that I wanted to explore the idea of becoming an author.

Also part of the origanal idea was that we would also potray the naemean lion as a Joe Biden type character.

Building & publishing the book

After coming up with our initial idea, the next step was to develop the story. We used ChatGPT to help write the book, then created illustrations to bring it to life in the style of a classic Ladybird book.

Once we had developed the story and illustrations, we needed to prepare the book for publication. We typed up and edited the text in Microsoft Word before preparing it for Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP).

If you want to publish your own book, the first step is to create a Kindle Direct Publishing account , unless you already have one.

Next, prepare your manuscript and upload it to KDP. You will need to enter information about your book, including its title and description, and decide whether you want to publish it as an eBook, a printed book, or both. If you choose to publish a printed book, you can use your own ISBN or, where available, a free ISBN provided by KDP.

Once your manuscript and cover are ready, you can preview the book to check that the text, illustrations and page layout look correct. You can then choose your publishing territories, set the price and review the available royalty options.

Finally, check all your details carefully and submit your book for publication. After KDP has reviewed and approved it, your book can become available for readers to purchase.

Problems & improvements

Compared with some of our other projects, Trumpales did not involve too many major problems. However, we did encounter a couple of challenges during the publishing process.

The first problem was formatting the book for Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). When we uploaded our work, some of the formatting was incorrect, which meant we had to spend time adjusting the layout to make sure everything looked right.

The second challenge was getting the illustrations to the correct size for the style and layout of the book. This took some time to sort out, as we needed to make sure the pictures fitted properly on each page.

The main lesson we learned from these challenges was the importance of organising and preparing files carefully. By resizing the illustrations correctly and saving them in clearly organised folders, it would be much easier to format the book for KDP and avoid similar problems in future projects.
